Testing caveats
---------------
- stHDX oracle REVERTS on lark 1: AaveOracle.getAssetPrice(stHDX) reverts
  because upstream substrate oracle feeds (stHDX/HDX 10min EMA, HDX/USD)
  aren't populated. Blocks supply/borrow until runtime/oracle team pushes
  data.
- HOLLAR oracle returns $1 fine.
- stHDX balances live on the substrate side (asset 670). EVM calls
  (decimals, balanceOf, transfer) dispatch to substrate asset registry.
- LockableAToken transfers call the 0x0806 precompile; zero-locked users
  transfer freely.
